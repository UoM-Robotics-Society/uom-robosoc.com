Design, fabricate, and control a multi-axis robotic manipulator.

The ARM Project focuses on precision kinematics, mechanical fabrication, and embedded actuation. Working in dedicated sub-teams, members engineer a functional robotic arm capable of dexterous grasping, coordinate-based trajectory planning, and automated pick-and-place tasks.

**What You'll Work On:**

Mechanical Design & Kinematics: Design multi-degree-of-freedom (DoF) joints, gear reduction setups, and rigid structural links while learning forward and inverse kinematics.

Actuation & Motor Control: Interface high-torque servos, stepper motors, and feedback encoders to ensure smooth, accurate multi-axis movement.

End-Effector Engineering: Prototype custom mechanical grippers, vacuum cups, or specialized tooling to handle various payloads.

Control Systems & Automation: Program inverse kinematic solvers, motion profiles, and automated routines for precise positional control.


**Experience Level:** Intermediate to Advanced. Best suited for students interested in mechanical kinematics, motor control algorithms, and industrial automation.
