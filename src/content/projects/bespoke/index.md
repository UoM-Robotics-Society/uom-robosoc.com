Bring your own custom robotics ideas to life with dedicated society support and resources.

The Bespoke Project is RoboSoc’s open-ended innovation incubator. Rather than following a fixed challenge curriculum, members or self-formed teams pitch their own original engineering ideas, from automated drones and robotic instruments to smart home automation and receive technical mentorship, lab workspace, and component funding to build them.

**What You'll Work On:**

Project Scoping & Pitching: Define your project roadmap, component bills of materials (BOM), and technical milestones.

Rapid Prototyping: Leverage the society’s 3D printers, laser cutters, hand tools, and testing benches to construct custom hardware.

Full-Stack Engineering: Combine mechanics, embedded microcontrollers, PCB design, and custom software tailored to your specific application.

Showcase Presentation: Demonstrate your working prototype at society events, hackathons, and end-of-year exhibitions.

**Experience Level:** All levels welcome. Whether you are building an ambitious final year passion project or testing an experimental prototype for fun, Bespoke gives you the platform to make it happen.
