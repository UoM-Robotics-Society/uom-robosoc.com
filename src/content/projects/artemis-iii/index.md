Engineer an autonomous planetary exploration rover built to navigate hostile simulated terrain.

Inspired by space exploration missions, Project Artemis tasks teams with designing, building, and programming an all-terrain rover capable of environmental sensing, obstacle traversal, and semi-autonomous waypoint navigation.

**What You'll Work On:**

Chassis & Suspension: Design durable rocker-bogie or multi-wheel suspension setups in CAD to climb uneven obstacles and loose terrain.

Embedded Electronics & Power: Integrate onboard power management, distributed sensors, and robust communication links for remote telemetry.

Autonomy & Navigation: Combine IMUs, GPS/local positioning, and computer vision or LiDAR to plot safe traversal paths.

Scientific Payloads: Prototype onboard soil-sampling mechanisms, environmental sensor arrays, or robotic tool arms.


**Experience Level:** Advanced. Ideal for students interested in aerospace engineering, robotics mobility systems, computer vision, and autonomous vehicle design.
