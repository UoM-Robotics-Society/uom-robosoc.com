Engineer an autonomous, transforming aerial-to-ground exploration vehicle built to navigate hostile simulated terrain. 

Inspired by space exploration missions, Project Artemis tasks teams with designing, building, and programming a hybrid drone-rover capable of aerial reconnaissance, environmental sensing, obstacle traversal, and seamless multi-modal waypoint navigation.

**What You'll Work On:**

Airframe & Chassis: Design lightweight, durable folding mechanisms in CAD that can withstand flight dynamics while incorporating multi-wheel suspension setups to climb uneven obstacles and loose terrain.

Embedded Electronics & Power: Integrate hybrid power management capable of handling both high-thrust flight and efficient ground rolling. Configure flight controllers, distributed sensors, and robust communication links for remote telemetry.

Autonomy & Navigation: Combine IMUs, GPS/local positioning, and computer vision to plot 3D flight paths and safe ground traversal routes. You will tackle the complex autonomous transition between flying and driving.

Scientific Payloads: Prototype onboard soil sampling mechanisms, aerial environmental sensor arrays, or lightweight robotic tool arms.

**Experience Level:** Advanced. Ideal for students interested in aerospace engineering, robotics mobility systems, computer vision, flight dynamics, and autonomous vehicle design.
