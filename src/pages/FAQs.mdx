Can't find your question below? Reach out to us at uomrobosoc2@gmail.com!

### Is it free to join a project?
Yes! The society is completely free to join, all projects are funded by us!

### Where do I go for a project?
Projects will be always be held in the Nancy Rothwell Building. Watch our socials for room announcements every week. 

### Can I just join for the socials?
No problem! You don't have to do a project to be a part of the society. Keep updated on our events on our [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/uom_robosoc/).

### Do I need technical experience to join?
Nope! We host some projects that require no experience whatsover, anyone is free to join and learn from following our step-by-step manuals. We also have Project Leads and Supports available to help you if you're stuck, so there's no need to worry. :)
However, our more intermediate and advanced projects, we do prefer that you have some coding or hardware experience depending on which project you want to pursue.
