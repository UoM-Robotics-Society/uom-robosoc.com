We will be running many events this year, including multiple hackathon events, culminating to a main Hack-A-Bot scheduled for March 2027 (more details to follow). We also host weekly sessions every Wednesday for different Projects, which you can find on the Projects Tab above. We also host social events every week, like Pub Crawls, Pumpkin Carving, Games Nights and many more! Make sure to get involved!

### Women In Robotics

Robotics should be for everyone. Our Women in Robotics events give women and non-binary students a welcoming space to get hands-on with hardware, meet others with the same interests and hear from people already working in the field. Expect beginner-friendly build sessions, talks and panels with engineers and researchers, and plenty of chances to ask the questions you've always wanted to ask. No experience needed: just bring your curiosity.

### Hack-A-Bot 2027

Hack-A-Bot is our flagship event: a 24-hour hardware hackathon where teams design, build and program a robot from scratch. Last year more than 350 people took part, making it one of the largest hardware hackathons in the UK. Beginners and experts are both welcome. We provide the components, tools, mentors and food, and you bring the ideas (and the stamina). Hack-A-Bot 2027 is coming in March, and more details will be announced soon, so keep an eye on our socials and on our website: https://hackabot2027.com/

### Accelerate ME

Together with Accelerate ME, the University's student-led start up accelerator, we run the hardware track of their fast-paced student hackathon. Teams get just a few hours to go from idea to working prototype across robotics, IoT and more, with mentors on hand to help. It's a great way to try your first hackathon or to see how your hardware skills could turn into a start up.

### Scalpel Hackathon

Robotics is changing surgery, and this hackathon puts you right where engineering meets medicine. Run with Scalpel, the University of Manchester Surgical Society, it brings engineers, computer scientists and medical students together to tackle real clinical challenges. You'll work in mixed teams, get insight from clinicians and pitch your solution to a panel of judges.

### Workshops

Throughout the year we run hands-on workshops covering the core skills behind every robot, including soldering, electronics, CAD, 3D printing and more. They're designed to get you from zero to building as quickly as possible, and they're the perfect warm up for our projects. All equipment is provided.

### Helsinki Robotics Week

In November 2026 RoboSoc went international for the first time, travelling to Finland for Helsinki Robotics Week, hosted by Aalto Robotics Club. Across the week we toured companies such as Nokia, GIM Robotics and Beckhoff, visited Aalto University's research labs, and ran our first workshop abroad: over 70 participants built and programmed our Ara-Crab walking robot with Atech. We finished the week by competing at Junction 2026, one of Europe's largest hackathons. We hope it's the first of many trips!

### Social Events

Robotics isn't all soldering irons and debugging. We run socials every week so you can get to know the rest of the society away from the workbench. Past socials include pub crawls, pumpkin carving, games nights, film nights and more. They're the easiest way to meet new people, whether you're on a project or just want to hang out!

### Sponsorships

None of our events would be possible without the support of our sponsors, who fund our components, competitions and hackathons, and help connect our members with industry. Thank you to everyone who supports us this year! If your company would like to work with us, please get in touch with our Sponsorship Director.
